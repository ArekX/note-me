# 0.87 Media alignment

- Decided by: user (left, center or right for images, audio and video from
  a small popup when that one item is selected; images through an
  extension, audio and video through a parameter); Claude (details below)
- Date: 2026-10-21
- Extends 0.82 (audio and video) and 0.86 (rich editor).

- Audio and video take an optional alignment as their second parameter:
  `{:audio|<URL>|center}`. Left is the default and is not written.
- An aligned image is the new `{:image|<URL>|<ALIGN>|<DESCRIPTION>}`
  extension, since markdown images have no alignment. Its address follows
  the rule markdown images go through (`isAllowedImageUrl`, markdown-it's
  `validateLink`), so `javascript:` and the like show an error box.
  Aligning a plain `![alt](src)` to center or right turns it into the
  extension and aligning it back to left turns it back into a plain image
  (an image title is dropped). `|`, `}` and line breaks are kept out of the
  parameters (`%7C`/`%7D` in the address, spaces in the description).
- An aligned item sits on its own line and is placed within it; text does
  not flow around it.
- In the rich editor `AlignToolbar` shows Left / Center / Right above the
  item only while a single image, audio or video is node-selected; a text
  selection over several items shows nothing. It is hidden while saving.
- The source editor offers `image` in the extension autocomplete; the
  markdown syntax help lists the alignment and the image extension.
