- Decided by: earlier sessions (moved from TODO.md section 0)

Kept the divergence and documented
it in `CLAUDE.md`: `DATABASE_DSN` replaces `SQLITE_DATABASE_LOCATION`;
`WEBSOCKET_PORT` and `SOCKET_HOSTNAME` are gone (websocket is on `/ws`
of the same port); `RUN_MIGRATIONS_ON_STARTUP`,
`DISABLE_SERVICE_PERIODIC_TASKS` and `ALLOWED_SERVICE_RETRIES` do not
exist and are not added back. The original's backup task reads
`MAX_BACKUP_COUNT` while its `.env.defaults` and UI use
`MAX_ALLOWED_BACKUP_COUNT` (original bug); the rewrite reads the latter.
`FILES_LOCATION` is new.
