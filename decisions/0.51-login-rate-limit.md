# 0.51 Login rate limit

- Decided by: Claude (TODO item 15, applying 0.9.s)
- Date: 2026-10-19

- `internal/components/loginguard` (on `components.LoginGuard`) counts
  attempts per client address in memory, as the original: more than 30
  attempts with less than 60 s between them lock the address for 900 s, and
  every attempt while locked extends the lock. Answer: 403 "Too many login
  requests, please try again later.". A successful sign in clears the
  address (the original never did). Entries idle for the cooldown are swept
  at most once a minute.
- Counted: `POST /api/login`, `POST /api/login/passkey/options`,
  `POST /api/login/passkey` (a passkey sign in is two attempts) and the
  password check before registering a passkey (0.50).
- Client address (`internal/http/clientip`): the connection's address,
  unless that is a trusted proxy from the new `TRUSTED_PROXIES` setting
  (comma separated addresses or CIDR ranges, none by default). Then
  `X-Forwarded-For` is walked from the right and the first address that is
  not a trusted proxy is the client; the entries left of it are whatever
  the client sent and are ignored. This is how "first hop only" of 0.9.s is
  read: the first hop outside our own proxies. `CF-Connecting-IP` is no
  longer read.
