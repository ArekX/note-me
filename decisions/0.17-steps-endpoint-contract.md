# 0.17 Steps endpoint contract

- Decided by: Claude (details of 0.12)
- Date: 2026-10-19

- `POST /api/process-step` runs on the session chain. Registry in
  `internal/http/routes/steps.go`: `step → action → {requiresPending, handle}`.
  Required steps come from `requeststate.Session.RequiredSteps()`.
- Unknown step or action: 400 `Unknown step or action.`; action of a required
  step that is not pending: 400 `This step is not pending for the user.`;
  validation and user errors (`New password must be different from the old
  password.`, `Old password is incorrect`): 400 `{error}`.
- On success every session row of the user is rewritten and the response is
  the `/api/session` payload.
- The step gate (`UserStepsRequiredMiddleware`) answers 403
  `{error: "Required step must be completed first", code: "step_required",
  step}` with the first pending step.
- Frontend: `stepRoutes` in `router/guards.ts` maps a step to its page; the
  guard sends users with a pending step there and sends users without one
  away from step pages (the original redirected `/app/reset-password` to
  `/app` the same way).
